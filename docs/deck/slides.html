02The problem

101 million Indians live with diabetes.Between visits, no one sees the spike coming.

ICMR-INDIAB-17 national survey: Anjana RM et al., Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology 2023.

Sparse data

Glucose moves every few minutes; clinic labs arrive every few months. Wearables and CGM fill the gap, but nobody can watch the stream.

Different phenotypes

Asian Indian T2D splits into SIDD, SIRD, MARD and IROD subgroups, plus the “thin-fat” phenotype2. One model does not fit all.

Different plates

Carbohydrate-heavy meals, evening chai and snacks, late dinners, festival sweets: the spikes follow the Indian day.

Different risks

Sulfonylureas are widely prescribed. On a fasting day (Navratri, Ekadashi, Ramadan) a missed meal plus an SU dose is a classic hypoglycaemia trap.

2 INSPIRED cohort: Anjana RM et al., BMJ Open Diabetes Research & Care 2020.

03Healthcare use case

A clinician following patients on CGMneeds to know who needs attention now, and why.

1

Triage

The cohort view ranks every synced twin by calibrated risk: level-2 hyperglycaemia within 2 h, hypoglycaemia within 1 h, nocturnal hypo tonight.

2

Understand

Open the virtual patient: CGM against the twin, a 4-hour forecast cone, and the reasons behind each risk (“time since sulfonylurea dose”).

3

Counsel

Test advice on this patient’s twin first: millet roti instead of rice, a walk after dinner, a skipped or shifted glimepiride dose, a short night.

The brief’s example

“Predict a sudden glucose spike 2 hours in advance” is our hyper_2h task. We add hypoglycaemia within 1 h and nocturnal hypoglycaemia tonight, because lows are what make sulfonylurea and insulin therapy dangerous.

04Solution overview

Record, sense, mirror, foresee.One twin per patient, updated every five minutes.

1

Record

The EHR (FHIR R4 with an ABHA-style ID) sets the twin’s priors: HbA1c and fasting glucose for baseline levels, the TyG index for insulin resistance, diabetes duration for β-cell reserve.

2

Sense

CGM every 5 minutes, heart rate, HRV, steps and sleep stages stream in with logged Indian meals and doses.

3

Mirror

A 64-member Ensemble Kalman Filter keeps a glucose–insulin model in sync, re-estimating insulin sensitivity, β-cell response and liver glucose output.

4

Foresee

The synced twin forecasts 4 hours ahead; a LightGBM layer turns it into calibrated, explained alerts and the What-If Lab tests advice.

The virtual patient in the clinician console: CGM dots, twin band (p10–p90), forecast cone to +4 h, calibrated risk with TreeSHAP reasons.

05What makes it a twin

Not a classifier with a dashboard.A model of this patient that stays in sync.

Mechanistic core

Physiology, not just patterns

An extended Bergman minimal model with gut absorption, exercise, sleep, stress, dawn effect and five Indian-common drug classes, personalised from the EHR.

Continuous sync

Re-estimated every 5 minutes

An Ensemble Kalman Filter updates hidden physiology from each CGM reading: insulin sensitivity, β-cell response, liver glucose output, meal absorption speed, dawn effect.

Counterfactuals

Answers “what if?”

The same synced ensemble simulates alternatives (meals, walks, doses, sleep) and returns baseline-vs-scenario bands, peaks, time above 180 / below 70 and hypo probability.

07The model

A compact physiological twin,personalised from the health record.

Twin state-space model · 1-min step, 5-min observations
dG/dt = −(SG + X)·G + EGP·fEGP(t) + Ra(t)/VG − kex·e(t)·G − R(G)
dX/dt = −p2·X + p2·SI·msleep·mstress·mex(t)·I
dI/dt = −n·I + β·max(G − h, 0)·drug(t) + Iexo(t)/VI
gut:  D1 → D2 → Ra,  τ from GI, fat, protein, fibre
dGi/dt = (G − Gi)/τi  (CGM sees Gi, τi ≈ 9 min)
  • Drugs: metformin lowers EGP · sulfonylurea adds insulin secretion, part of it glucose-independent · DPP-4 boosts post-meal secretion · SGLT2i lowers the renal threshold 180 → 105 mg/dL · glargine as a slow depot
  • Wearables: a short night lowers SI by up to 20 % next day · stress (HR up, HRV down vs 7-day baseline) raises EGP · steps drive exercise uptake
EHR → priors for θ = {SI, SG, β, EGP, meal rate, dawn}
SI insulin sensitivityTyG index, waist/height, BMI
β β-cell responseduration, HbA1c, C-peptide, TCF7L2
EGP liver outputfasting glucose + ADAG eAG
SG, meal rate, dawnpopulation priors
Formulas used

TyG = ln(TG [mg/dL] × FPG [mg/dL] / 2)
eAG (mg/dL) = 28.7 × HbA1c − 46.7

Honest note: the filtered twin is slightly less accurate than raw CGM against truth (9.38 % vs 8.51 % MARD) because the EnKF cannot remove correlated sensor drift. Its value is in forecasting and in the features it feeds the risk models.

How the sync works
  • 64 members; augmented state = 7 dynamic states + 6 parameters (log / logit space, slow random walk)
  • Update on each CGM reading (obs. sd 5 + 6 %); missing CGM → no update
  • 4σ innovation gate rejects compression artefacts; inflation 1.001
  • Forecast 240 min with a habitual-meal prior; intervals widened by validation-fitted factors

09Risk layer & explainability

Three early warnings,each with its reasons.

hyper_2h

Level-2 hyperglycaemia (>250 mg/dL for ≥15 min) begins within the next 120 min

hypo_1h

Hypoglycaemia (<70 mg/dL for ≥15 min) begins within the next 60 min

nocturnal_hypo

Any hypo between 00:00 and 06:00 tonight, predicted at bedtime

Labels come from hidden truth plasma glucose, never from CGM artefacts; a CGM-labelled variant is reported too.

Pipeline (every 15 min)
  • ~100 strictly causal features (data ≤ t): CGM dynamics, carbs on board, meds, steps, HR/HRV, sleep, EHR, plus 33 twin features
  • LightGBM per task · isotonic calibration on validation patients
  • TreeSHAP (pred_contrib) → plain-language drivers
  • Alerts: threshold for ≤ 1 false alert per patient-day (nocturnal ≤ 1 per patient-week), cool-down + hysteresis

14Results · honest limits

What the real data told us,and what we change next.

Findings
  • A data-driven forecaster beats the physiological twin at every horizon (14.06 / 21.7 / 27.87 vs 27.3 / 30.84 / 34.51 mg/dL).
  • Twin features add no measurable discrimination on real data (≥180: 0.8681 → 0.8703; >250: 0.9232 → 0.9235 AUROC).
  • The synthetic-trained model transfers poorly (AUROC 0.7902 for >250, 0.6628 for ≥180).
  • Our synthetic cohort is too sick: 2.479× the real ≥180 event rate, 5.439× the >250 rate and 1.532× the 30-min volatility.
Caveats & next steps
  • 87 people from China and the USA, not India; insulin users excluded; no waist, sleep or HRV; meals are photo or text estimates.
  • Recalibrate the simulator to real event rates and volatility, then retrain.
  • Anchor short horizons to the CGM and blend the twin with a data-driven forecaster.
  • Prospective validation on Indian patients before any clinical claim.

17India-specific design

Built for India,not translated to it.

ABDM-ready records

Every synthetic patient is an HL7 FHIR R4 Bundle, the format of the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, with an ABHA-format identifier, LOINC labs and SNOMED CT conditions.

73 Indian foods

Katori / roti / piece portions with Hindi names, carbs, fibre, GI and GL from IFCT 2017 and the International GI Tables, with an honest source column.

सफ़ेद चावल · GI 73बाजरे की रोटी · GI 55पोहा · GI 70इडली · GI 69राजमा · GI 24चाय (चीनी वाली)
INSPIRED subtypes

Cohort archetypes from Asian Indian T2D subgroups: IROD ~39 %, SIDD ~26 %, MARD ~22 %, SIRD ~13 %, including the “thin-fat” phenotype and Asian BMI cut-offs (23 / 25 kg/m²).

Cohort: IROD 96 · SIDD 65 · MARD 46 · SIRD 33

Sulfonylurea safety

Fasting days with an SU dose are simulated. 97 % of hypo episodes occur in SU / insulin users, and “time since sulfonylurea dose” is a top driver of hypo alerts. The What-If Lab shows what skipping or shifting a dose does.

Late dinners & chai

Late dinners (≈ 20:00–22:45), evening chai + snack, festival sweets, region-flavoured meal templates. Median post-meal rise: lunch 122.1, dinner 111.8, breakfast 95.5 mg/dL.

Routine labs only

Fasting insulin is rarely measured in India, so insulin resistance comes from the TyG index (triglycerides × fasting glucose), and baseline glucose from HbA1c via ADAG.

18Limitations & ethics

What we do not claim,and how we keep it safe.

Limitations
  • Trained on synthetic data only. The real-data check shows synthetic accuracy does not carry over; prospective validation is required before any claim.
  • The truth simulator is richer than the twin but from the same minimal-model family; real physiology will be further away.
  • Posterior SI and β are “effective” values: use them as trends, not lab measurements.
  • Hypo alerts have low precision (0.0794 for hypo_1h) because lows are rare; nocturnal metrics rest on 8 positive nights.
  • Food GI values are approximate; meal logging is assumed timely.
Safety & ethics
  • Not a medical device. Alerts and recommendations are illustrative; the clinician decides.
  • Training data are synthetic (names, ABHA-format IDs); real data only as open, de-identified datasets for an external check (DPDP Act 2023 / HIPAA sandbox).
  • TreeSHAP drivers explain the model, not the patient’s biology: “explanations, not causes”.
  • The inferred subtype agrees with the hidden archetype 50.8 % of the time: a rough phenotype, never a diagnosis.
  • No LLM in the loop: forecasts, risks and what-if narratives are deterministic and reproducible.
Intended use

A conceptual clinician console for triage and counselling, demonstrated on synthetic patients for this challenge.

Out of scope

Any clinical decision or dosing, identifiable patient data, paediatric use, type 1 diabetes, pregnancy.
