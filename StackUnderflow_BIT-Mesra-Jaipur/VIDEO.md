# Video walkthrough

**Pratibimb: a hybrid physiology + ML digital twin for Type 2 Diabetes**
Team Stack Underflow · BIT Mesra, Off Campus Jaipur · Happiest Health Digital Twin Challenge 2026

- Video (20+ minutes): **[https://youtu.be/3gu_ek68Mm0](https://youtu.be/3gu_ek68Mm0)** (unlisted YouTube, 22:57; MP4 + captions mirror: [GitHub release](https://github.com/Thanatos9404/pratibimb/releases/tag/submission-video))
- Live demo: **https://pratibimb-omega.vercel.app**

The video covers the problem and healthcare use case, the system architecture, the synthetic data and sandbox compliance, the twin model and its EnKF sync, the risk layer and explanations, the evaluation results, the external validation on real open patient data (CGMacros and ShanghaiT2DM), and a walkthrough of the clinician console and What-If Lab.

Training data and the console demo use synthetic patients; the real-data validation uses open, de-identified datasets (CGMacros, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; ShanghaiT2DM, CC BY 4.0). Pratibimb is not a medical device.
